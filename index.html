eBay Agent Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

eBay Agent is a seller-assistance application that connects to eBay only after the account owner authorizes access.

Information we process

The application may process eBay account identifiers, OAuth authorization tokens, listings, inventory, business policies, orders, fulfillment information, and related marketplace data required to provide the service.

How information is used

Information is used only to create and manage listings, synchronize orders, support fulfillment, calculate and display marketplace information, and operate requested automation. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and security

Authorization credentials and application data are stored with access controls appropriate to their sensitivity. Access is limited to operation of the application.

Account deletion and eBay notifications

We honor eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications. When eBay notifies the application that an account is deleted or closed, associated personal data is deleted or anonymized as required, except information that must be retained for legal or security purposes.

Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers necessary to operate the application and with eBay as required for the requested marketplace functions. We do not disclose data for advertising resale.

Your choices

You may revoke eBay authorization at any time through your eBay account permissions. You may also request deletion of application-held personal data.
